Our Purpose and Mission

DOCUMENT

Uncovering the Truth

Our primary objective is to meticulously document the incidents, testimonies, and systemic issues that expose Zalando's alleged discriminatory behavior towards pro-Palestine supporters, especially those from Muslim backgrounds. We conduct thorough investigations and periodically post accounts that reveal Zalando’s Zionist and Facist stance. This includes:

  • Collecting and analyzing anonymous testimonies from current and former employees detailing instances of repression, intimidation, and silencing.
  • Highlighting specific cases, such as the pressure to remove Palestinian symbolism like the watermelon emoji under threat of termination.
  • Uncovering the patterns that suggest a systemic, premeditated, and organized effort to intimidate employees out of their Palestinian solidarity, rather than isolated incidents.
  • Investigating the company's policies, such as the alleged ban of culturally significant items like the Keffiyeh pattern, demonizing Palestinian identity itself.
View Employee Testimonies

AMPLIFY

Spreading Awareness

Once documented, our crucial next step is to amplify these findings to the widest possible audience. We believe that transparency and public awareness are key to holding Zalando accountable. Our efforts include:

  • Publicizing the results of our investigation, clearly showing the alleged Islamophobia and anti-Palestine sentiment plaguing Zalando's higher ranks, which we believe they try to hide behind a facade of "Fostering Diversity & Inclusion".
  • Utilizing platforms like this website and social media, leveraging hashtags such as #ExposeZalando and #BoycottZalando, to reach customers, shareholders, and the general public.
  • Highlighting the company's perceived incompetence and mismanagement due to its aggressive political stance, especially given its customer-centric business model and competitive market.
  • Drawing attention to the financial ties and influence of major shareholders and political figures that may contribute to Zalando's alleged anti-Palestine posture.
Share Our Posts

MOBILIZE

Get Involved Today

Finally, our ultimate goal is to mobilize collective action. We seek to empower individuals and organizations to act in solidarity with affected employees and the Palestinian cause, for example, through the "Delete Zalando Day" Campaign. We demand concrete changes from Zalando, including:

  • An immediate halt to the persecution of employees displaying Palestine solidarity symbols or engaging in pro-Palestine activism.
  • A public, unequivocal apology for the recent incidents and the broader culture of repression, discrimination, and silence the company has fostered.
  • A transparent review and overhaul process to fundamentally dismantle the toxic, fear-based hierarchical culture that allows discrimination and repression to flourish.
  • Reparations for those harmed—including reinstatement, compensation, and support for employees unjustly disciplined or dismissed because of their religious identity, political beliefs, or cultural expression.
